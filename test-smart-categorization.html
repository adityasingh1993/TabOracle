🚀 How to Test Smart Categorization

  • Reload the Extension: Go to chrome://extensions/ and click the refresh button on TabOracle
  • Open the Popup: Click the TabOracle icon in your toolbar
  • Go to Category Search: Click the "Category Search" tab
  • See Smart Categories: Tabs are automatically categorized with confidence scores
  • Click Categories: See detailed breakdown with keywords and tab lists
  • Navigate to Tabs: Click on any tab to switch to it
