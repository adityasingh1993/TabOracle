# Privacy Policy for TabOracle Chrome Extension

**Last updated:** August 16, 2024

## Introduction

TabOracle ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome extension.

## Information We Collect

### What We Do NOT Collect
- **Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information
- **Browsing History**: We do not track your browsing history or web activity
- **Tab Content**: We do not store the actual content of your web pages
- **Search Queries**: We do not log or store your search queries
- **User Behavior**: We do not track how you use the extension

### What We Do Collect (Locally Only)
- **Tab Metadata**: Basic information about your open tabs (title, URL, domain)
- **Extension Settings**: Your preferences and settings for the extension
- **Performance Data**: Basic performance metrics to improve the extension

## How We Use Information

### Local Processing Only
All information processing happens **locally on your device**. We do not:
- Send any data to external servers
- Share information with third parties
- Use your data for advertising or marketing
- Analyze your browsing patterns

### Extension Functionality
We use the collected information only to:
- Provide tab search and categorization features
- Improve extension performance
- Remember your preferences
- Enable the core functionality of TabOracle

## Data Storage

### Local Storage
All data is stored **locally on your device** using:
- Chrome's local storage API
- Extension's internal memory
- No external databases or cloud storage

### Data Retention
- Tab metadata is stored only while tabs are open
- Settings are retained until you uninstall the extension
- No data persists after extension removal

## Permissions Required

### Chrome Extension Permissions
TabOracle requests the following permissions:

- **`tabs`**: To read basic tab information (title, URL, domain)
- **`activeTab`**: To access the currently active tab
- **`storage`**: To save your preferences locally
- **`scripting`**: To analyze page content for categorization
- **`host_permissions`**: To access web page content for analysis

### Why These Permissions Are Needed
- **Tab Management**: Essential for the core functionality
- **Content Analysis**: Required for intelligent categorization
- **Local Storage**: Needed to remember your settings
- **Performance**: Enables fast, responsive search

## Third-Party Services

### No External Services
TabOracle does not:
- Integrate with third-party analytics
- Use external AI or machine learning services
- Connect to cloud-based APIs
- Share data with other companies

### Future Considerations
If we add features that require external services (such as AI-powered categorization), we will:
- Update this privacy policy
- Provide clear opt-in/opt-out options
- Ensure data remains secure and private

## Data Security

### Local Processing
- All data processing happens on your device
- No data transmission over the internet
- No risk of data breaches from external servers

### Chrome Security
- Extension runs within Chrome's secure environment
- Follows Chrome's security best practices
- Regular security updates and maintenance

## Children's Privacy

TabOracle is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
