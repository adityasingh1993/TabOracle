# TabOracle Privacy Policy (Draft)

TabOracle runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your browsing data to our servers.

- Data sources: titles, URLs, and page content of your open tabs are accessed locally to provide search and summaries.
- AI processing: On-device model (Chrome Language Model) or local algorithms are used. No cloud calls are made by the extension.
- PDFs: When summarizing PDFs, TabOracle may download the current PDF or, if you enable the optional fallback, temporarily attach a local debugging session to capture the PDF bytes for parsing. This occurs only when you click "Generate Summary" on a PDF page and detaches immediately after. No data leaves your device.
- Storage: Minimal settings (e.g., PDF capture fallback toggle) may be stored in chrome.storage.local.

If you have questions or requests, please contact the developer.
